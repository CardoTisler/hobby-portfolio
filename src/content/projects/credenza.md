A low credenza with slatted wooden sliding doors. The interior is divided into four sections - outermost two sections
are full height, for storing things like boardgames, and middle sections are split in half for storing electronic devices & controllers/remotes, with pill-shaped cuts made in the
veneer allowing wires to be passed in from behind.

The legs, tabletop and half the slats are solid walnut. Carcass and the other half
of slats are solid ash and the back panel is ash-veneered plywood.
